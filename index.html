01 · About

A researcher on a mission for trustworthy, distributed AI

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS) in Montréal, advised by Prof. Kaiwen Zhang. My thesis, Fair and Secure Federated Adversarial Training, tackles a hard question: how do we train machine learning models that are simultaneously private, robust to attacks, fair across participants, and efficient with energy, even when they live across many devices.

Along the way I've built a decentralized federated learning framework with an enhanced global model and adaptive aggregator selection for network intrusion detection, a resource-aware hybrid federated adversarial training model, and an energy-aware adaptive attack model for mobile edge computing. Today I'm designing energy-aware attack strategies for adversarial training.

Before my PhD I spent years shipping applied deep learning, from medical imaging and agriculture to real-time computer vision, and teaching the next generation of computer scientists. I care about research that doesn't just publish, but holds up in the real world.

02 · Research

Where I focus

My work sits at the intersection of distributed machine learning and security & privacy, making collaborative AI both trustworthy and practical.

🔐

Privacy-Preserving AI

Learning from sensitive, decentralized data without ever exposing it, keeping models useful and people protected.

🤝

Federated & Distributed ML

Decentralized training, adaptive aggregator selection, and enhanced global models across heterogeneous clients.

🛡️

Adversarial Training & Attacks

Hardening models against adversarial examples, then stress-testing them with adaptive, realistic attack strategies.

⚡

Energy-Aware ML

Making training and attacks resource-conscious for mobile edge computing, where every joule counts.

🧩

Split Learning

Partitioning models across parties to balance privacy, communication cost, and performance.

💬

Secure & Robust LLMs

Defense mechanisms for large language models in distributed settings, toward dependable, attack-resistant AI.
