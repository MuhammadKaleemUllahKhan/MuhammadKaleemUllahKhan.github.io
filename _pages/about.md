I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at [École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS)](https://www.etsmtl.ca/) in Montréal, advised by [Prof. Kaiwen Zhang](https://www.etsmtl.ca/en/research/professors/kaiwenzhang/). My thesis, *Fair and Secure Federated Adversarial Training*, asks a hard question: how do we train machine-learning models that are simultaneously **private, robust to attacks, fair across participants, and efficient with energy**, even when they live across many distributed devices.

Along the way I have built a decentralized federated learning framework with an enhanced global model and adaptive aggregator selection for network-intrusion detection, a resource-aware hybrid federated adversarial training model, and an energy-aware adaptive attack model for mobile edge computing. I am currently designing **energy-aware attack strategies for adversarial training**.

Before my PhD I spent several years shipping applied deep learning, from medical imaging and agriculture to real-time computer vision, and teaching the next generation of computer scientists.

**Research interests:** privacy-preserving AI, federated &amp; distributed machine learning, adversarial training and attacks, split learning, energy-aware ML, and secure &amp; robust large language models.
