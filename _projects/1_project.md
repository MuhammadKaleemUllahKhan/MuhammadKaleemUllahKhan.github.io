A decentralized federated learning framework that combines **adaptive aggregator selection** with an **enhanced global model** to speed convergence and improve robustness across heterogeneous, non-IID clients. Applied to network-intrusion detection, the approach removes the single central server as a bottleneck and point of failure. Published as *FL-EGM* at ICMLA 2024, with follow-up work at Middleware 2025.
